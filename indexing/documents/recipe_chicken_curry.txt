Chicken curry

Start by preparing all the ingredients for the chicken curry before turning on the stove. Let the chicken curry rest for ten minutes before serving. For the best chicken curry, use fresh ingredients from the local market.

This recipe for chicken curry takes about forty minutes from start to finish. Leftover chicken curry keeps well in the fridge for up to three days. My grandmother always added a pinch of nutmeg to her chicken curry.
