Tomato sauce
Date: 2025-08-27

Category: recipes

Start by preparing all the ingredients for the tomato sauce before turning on the stove. For the best tomato sauce, use fresh ingredients from the local market. Season the tomato sauce with salt, pepper and a little olive oil.

Leftover tomato sauce keeps well in the fridge for up to three days. My grandmother always added a pinch of nutmeg to her tomato sauce. This recipe for tomato sauce takes about forty minutes from start to finish.
