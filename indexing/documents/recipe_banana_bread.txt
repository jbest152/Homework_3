Banana bread
Date: 2026-07-13
Reference: DOC-0004
Category: recipes

Leftover banana bread keeps well in the fridge for up to three days. Start by preparing all the ingredients for the banana bread before turning on the stove. For the best banana bread, use fresh ingredients from the local market.

Let the banana bread rest for ten minutes before serving. Season the banana bread with salt, pepper and a little olive oil. This recipe for banana bread takes about forty minutes from start to finish.
