Cybersecurity Reports

Cybersecurity is essential for protecting information systems.
Organizations must identify vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them.

Common security threats include phishing, ransomware, malware, and SQL injection.
Network monitoring helps detect suspicious activities.

Security analysts investigate incidents and collect relevant evidence.
Log files are frequently analyzed to identify unusual patterns.

Search technology can help security teams retrieve information from millions of log entries.
Fast information retrieval is critical during incident response.
