Beef stew
Date: 2025-06-13
Reference: DOC-0005
Category: recipes

Leftover beef stew keeps well in the fridge for up to three days. Season the beef stew with salt, pepper and a little olive oil. Serve the beef stew warm with a side of crusty bread and a glass of water.

Start by preparing all the ingredients for the beef stew before turning on the stove. Let the beef stew rest for ten minutes before serving. For the best beef stew, use fresh ingredients from the local market.
