Lemon pasta
Date: 2025-04-25
Reference: DOC-0001
Category: recipes

This recipe for lemon pasta takes about forty minutes from start to finish. My grandmother always added a pinch of nutmeg to her lemon pasta. Season the lemon pasta with salt, pepper and a little olive oil.

Start by preparing all the ingredients for the lemon pasta before turning on the stove. Let the lemon pasta rest for ten minutes before serving. Serve the lemon pasta warm with a side of crusty bread and a glass of water.
