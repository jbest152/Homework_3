Natural Language Processing

Natural Language Processing enables computers to analyze human language.
NLP systems can identify words, sentences, entities, and semantic relationships.

Tokenization divides text into smaller units called tokens.
Stemming reduces words to their root forms.
Lemmatization identifies the dictionary form of a word.

For example, running, runs, and ran may be associated with the same concept.
Stop words such as the, is, and a are frequently removed during text analysis.

Natural language processing is widely used in search engines and chatbots.
